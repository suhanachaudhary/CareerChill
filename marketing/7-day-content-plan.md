## Reel scripts

**Day 1 — "Resume reject ho raha hai?"**
- 0–3s (hook, text on screen): "Resume reject ho raha hai? 😬"
- 3–20s: 3 quick text cards — (1) Photo/table/graphics that ATS can't read (2) No keywords from the job post (3) Duties listed, not achievements
- 20–28s: "Fix karwana hai? Link in bio — CareerChill kar lo."

**Day 2 — Fresher mistakes**
- Hook: "Fresher ho? Resume mein ye 3 galtiyan mat karna"
- (1) Objective paragraph jo kuch nahi batata (2) Projects without tech stack / outcome (3) 3 pages ka resume — fresher ke liye 1 page enough
- CTA: "Template nahi, personal resume chahiye? DM 'CHILL'."

**Day 4 — React**
- Hook: "React interview? Ye 5 cheezein revise kar lo"
- Virtual DOM · hooks (useState/useEffect) · props vs state · keys in lists · controlled vs uncontrolled inputs
- CTA: "Poora kit chahiye? Link in bio."

**Day 6 — Tell me about yourself**
- Formula: Present (who you are) → Past (relevant skills/projects) → Future (why this role). 30–45 seconds.

## Rules for content
1. Never say a question is a *guaranteed actual interview question*. Say "commonly practiced".
2. Verify company hiring patterns from official sources before publishing.
3. Before/after samples: customer permission + hide name, phone, email, address.
4. Track weekly: inquiries · paid orders · testimonials. (Google Sheet columns: Date, Name, Service, Source, Price, Paid?, Delivered?, Testimonial?)
